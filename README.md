# bioMérieux Equity Research and Valuation

## Project overview

This independent project evaluates **bioMérieux**, a European in-vitro diagnostics company, through company research, historical financial analysis, forecasting, discounted cash flow valuation, comparable-company analysis and an investment pitch.

The project connects a **Biological Sciences background** with an interest in banking, corporate finance and equity research.

## Research question

Can growth in molecular diagnostics, microbiology and recurring reagent and consumable sales support sustainable revenue growth, margin expansion and shareholder value?

## Planned deliverables

- Historical financial analysis
- Five-year financial forecast
- Discounted cash flow valuation model
- Comparable-company analysis
- Stock pitch deck

## Current stage

**Stage 5 — Comparable Company Analysis**

Current tasks:

- Select 5–8 relevant diagnostics and life-sciences peers and justify why each is comparable to bioMérieux.
- Compare valuation multiples including EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E and growth/margin profiles.
- Use peer median multiples to estimate bioMérieux’s implied enterprise value, equity value and share price, then compare this with the DCF.

## Key areas to analyse

- Revenue growth
- EBITDA and CEBIT margins
- Recurring consumables/reagent revenue
- R&D intensity
- Geographic exposure
- Net debt / net cash position
- EV/Revenue
- EV/EBITDA
- P/E
- Implied valuation range
- Premium or discount versus peers
- Differences in business mix and growth quality
