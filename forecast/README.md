# Five-Year Forecast

This workbook contains a five-year financial forecast for bioMérieux covering FY2026E to FY2030E.

## Contents

- Forecast assumptions
- Revenue forecast by business line
- Forecast operating results
- EBITDA, CEBIT, net income and EPS
- Capital expenditure and working-capital assumptions
- Unlevered free cash flow
- Forecast summary and charts

## Modelling Convention

- Blue font: hardcoded assumptions
- Black font: calculations
- Green font: links to other worksheets

The model is based on public information available as of 24 July 2026 and should be updated following subsequent company results.
